# CLAUDE.md template

Fill in the blanks, delete the hints, save it.

**Where it goes**
- Claude Code: save as `CLAUDE.md` in your project folder, or `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` to apply it everywhere.
- Claude app: paste it into Settings > Profile ("What personal preferences should Claude consider?") or into a Project's instructions.

Keep it short. Under 60 lines works best. Claude reads every line, every time.

---

```markdown
# About me

## Who I am
<Your role in one line. e.g. "Solo founder building an AI scheduling app.">
<Who you serve. e.g. "My customers are busy freelance designers.">

## What I'm working on right now
- <Project 1, one line>
- <Project 2, one line>

## How I want answers
- Lead with the answer. Reasons after, only if I need them.
- Short sentences. Plain English. No jargon unless I use it first.
- Give me one recommendation, not a menu of options.
- If you're unsure, say what you're unsure about in one line.

## My voice (for anything you write as me)
- <3 words that describe your tone. e.g. "direct, warm, a bit funny">
- I say: <phrases you actually use>
- I never say: <phrases you hate>
- Example of my writing: <paste 3 to 5 sentences you wrote>

## Rules
- Never use em dashes.
- Never use: delve, tapestry, leverage, game-changer, unlock, elevate.
- Ask before big edits, deleting anything, or sending anything.
- Use <USD / your currency> and <your units>.

## Context you should know
- Tools I use: <e.g. Notion, Gmail, Figma, Stripe>
- Files to read first: <e.g. /brand/voice.md, /offers.md>
```

---

**Test it:** start a fresh chat and ask "What do you know about me and how should you write for me?" If the answer is vague, your file is too vague.
