# Email reply

## Inputs
- The email I received.
- Optional: what I want to say, in a few words ("yes but next week", "politely no").

## Steps
1. Work out what the sender actually needs from me. One line.
2. If I gave no direction, ask me one question: what's my answer?
3. Draft the reply:
   - First line answers their question.
   - Max 5 sentences unless the email asks for detail.
   - One clear next step (a date, a link, a yes/no).
4. Give me the draft only. No commentary before or after it.

## Rules
- Match their formality. Casual email gets a casual reply.
- Write in my voice from CLAUDE.md.
- Never promise dates, prices or deliverables I didn't mention.
- Never send. Drafts only.
